# Changelog

## 1.2.1

- The default style now looks like the panels on the game's HUD.

## 1.1.0

- Two ways of counting DPS instead of four: the whole fight or the last few seconds. If you had one of the removed ones, you're back on the whole fight.
- Removed `Only health removed`, the window's toggle key and the reset button. F6 still resets it.
- Damage over time and companion damage moved to the main page.
- Settings that don't do anything right now are greyed out.
- Plainer names and descriptions in the menu.

## 1.0.1

- The window hides while a menu is open.
- The settings show the default's real name, not the internal one.
